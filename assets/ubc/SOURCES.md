## Campus Photograph

`vancouver-campus.jpg` is the unchanged Vancouver campus hero photograph
from UBC's campus page. UBC describes it as an aerial view of campus sports fields.

Page: https://www.ubc.ca/our-campuses/vancouver/

Image: https://www.ubc.ca/_assets/img/our-campuses/ubc_ca_image_our_campuses-vancouver-hero-1920x700.jpg

Source: The University of British Columbia. The source page does not name
an individual photographer. The theme clips the image at display time;
the original image file is retained.

The repository's CC BY 4.0 statement covers the template as documented in
the root README. UBC's logos and photograph remain UBC assets; their inclusion
does not assert a separate CC licence or official endorsement by UBC.
